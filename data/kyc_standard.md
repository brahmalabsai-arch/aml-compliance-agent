# KYC & Counterparty Verification Standard
Document ID: KYC-STD-2026 | Version 2.1 | Owner: Onboarding & Compliance

## Section 1 — Counterparty Identification
1.1 Before any payment is released to a new counterparty, the legal name,
registered country, and beneficial owner must be verified against the
institution's records.

1.2 If a counterparty's registered country differs from the destination country
of the payment, the transaction is automatically routed to REVIEW for
trade-based money laundering (TBML) risk assessment.

## Section 2 — Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
2.1 Any counterparty identified as a PEP, or owned/controlled by a PEP, requires
Enhanced Due Diligence regardless of transaction amount.

2.2 A PEP match must never be auto-approved. Minimum outcome is REVIEW.

## Section 3 — Ongoing Monitoring
3.1 Counterparties are re-screened against sanctions lists on every transaction,
not only at onboarding, because list membership changes over time.

3.2 A counterparty that was previously cleared but later appears on a sanctions
list must have all pending payments BLOCKED immediately.
